Educational Purpose
Learn • Practice • Understand
What this app is for

Unified Dose is designed for healthcare students, trainees, and professionals who want to learn and practice medication-dose, concentration, and infusion-rate calculations.

  • Understand the calculation formula.
  • Practice entering dose, weight, volume, concentration, and time.
  • Review step-by-step calculation logic.
  • Use the treatment library as an educational reference.
Clinical Safety Notice:
This application is for educational and calculation-practice purposes only. It must not be used as a substitute for prescribing information, product labeling, institutional protocols, or professional clinical judgment. Drug information and calculations must be independently verified before clinical use.

Privacy Policy
Unified Dose — educational clinical learning calculator
Data minimization

Unified Dose is designed to work offline. The calculator does not require a user account and is not designed to upload patient data to a remote server.

Information entered in the app

Values such as age, weight, dose, concentration, volume, time, and calculation settings are used locally to perform calculations and improve the user's learning workflow. Do not enter names, identifiers, or other unnecessary personal health information.

Storage

App preferences and locally created treatment data may be stored on the device using local browser/app storage. This data is not intentionally transmitted by the calculator to the developer.

Third-party services

The core calculator is designed to function without analytics, advertising, or health-data cloud services. If future versions add third-party services, this policy and the store disclosures must be updated before release.

Medical safety:
This application is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is an educational and calculation-practice tool. Always verify medication information, dose, concentration, route, maximum dose, compatibility, and institutional guidance before clinical use. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How to Use the App
Quick Tutorial

Unified Dose & Pump Calculator

A quick guide to selecting a treatment and calculating dose, volume, concentration, or infusion pump rate.

1
Start with Calculator

Open Calculator and choose the mode you need:

💊 Dose 💧 Pump 🧪 Concentration
2
Select a Treatment
  • Search for a treatment by name.
  • Select a treatment from the library.
  • Use generic or trade names when available.
  • Add new treatments from the Treatments section.
3
Enter Patient Age

Enter the patient's Age and select Years or Months. If age-based regimens are available, the app can use the age to display the relevant regimen.

4
Dose Mode

Use Dose Mode to calculate the required dose or volume.

  • Enter the prescribed dose and dose type.
  • Enter patient weight when the dose is weight-based.
  • Enter the available/final volume and concentration as required.
  • Select frequency when needed for daily and per-dose calculations.
Required Dose = Dose × Weight
Volume = Required Dose × Available Volume ÷ Concentration
5
Pump Mode

Use Pump Mode to calculate the infusion or syringe pump rate.

  • Enter the prescribed drug rate.
  • Enter patient weight when weight-based dosing is used.
  • Enter the final preparation volume.
  • Enter the total drug amount in the preparation.
  • Review the time conversion used by the calculator.
Pump Rate (mL/hr) = Required Drug Rate × Final Volume ÷ Total Drug Amount
6
Concentration Mode

Use Concentration Mode for concentration and volume conversions. Enter the old and new preparation information shown on the screen, then review the calculated result and steps.

7
Understand the Result
  • Required Dose — calculated drug dose.
  • Volume to Give — calculated administration volume.
  • Total Daily Dose — total daily amount when applicable.
  • Dose Each Time — amount per administration when frequency is available.
  • mL/hr — pump rate in Pump Mode.
8
Useful Actions
  • Edit Inputs — modify the calculation values.
  • Show Formula — display the calculation formula.
  • Clear All — clear the current inputs.
9
Manage Treatments
  • Add, edit, and save treatments.
  • Store doses, concentrations, volumes, and age regimens.
  • Import or export treatment data as JSON.
10
Settings
  • Light / Dark — change the application theme.
  • Auto Calculate — enable or disable automatic calculations.
  • Show Formula — show or hide the formula section.
  • Show Treatment Selector — show or hide treatment selection.
  • Show Patient Age — show or hide the patient age section.
